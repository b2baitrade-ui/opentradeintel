# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OpenTradeIntel a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing others' private information, and conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through a contact method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not place sensitive personal information in a public issue. All complaints will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as maintainer capacity permits, with respect for reporter privacy and safety.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal depending on impact and pattern of behavior. Decisions should prioritize community safety, proportionality, and an opportunity to correct behavior when appropriate.
